## 1. Brand Overview

**Mission:** give Filipino online shoppers one place they can trust to read and write blunt,
verified reviews, and be rewarded for honesty.

**Personality:** blunt, warm, on-your-side, Filipino, unpretentious.

**We are / We are not:**

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Blunt and honest ("Hard pass") | Diplomatic and hedging |
| Warm and local | Sterile marketplace gray |
| For the contributor | A faceless catalog |
| Trust-first | Volume-first |

## 2. Voice & Tone

- Say it plainly. The product is honesty, so the copy never buries the verdict.
- Taglish-friendly and human. "Sabihin mo nang tapat." Encourage the reviewer; never gate them behind decoration.
- No stock enthusiasm, no jargon. Short, direct sentences.

## 3. Logo

- Primary wordmark and icon mark live in `public/brand/` (export from Figma `Icons & Logos`).
- Clear space: 1x mark height. Minimum mark height: 20px.
- Approved backgrounds: white, `#F2F2F2`, dark `#141414`, or the brand gradient with sufficient contrast.
- Don'ts: recolor outside the palette, stretch, drop-shadow, place over a busy photo without a scrim.

## 4. Color (verbal)

Warm orange is the single committed accent (`#EF782D`, deepening to `#96371A`). Marketing
surfaces are confident dark (`#141414`); the app is light (`#FFFFFF` / `#F2F2F2`) with orange
reserved for primary actions and active states. Verdicts speak in color: green for "yes",
amber for "it depends", red for "hard pass". Full values in `DESIGN.md`.

## 5. Typography (verbal)

Poppins throughout: SemiBold for headlines, Regular for body. Friendly, rounded, legible on
mobile. Never orange body text on white (fails contrast); see `DESIGN.md`.

## 6. Imagery & Illustration

Flat editorial spot illustrations in the warm palette, depicting everyday Filipino shopping
moments (the payday-sale scroll, two people talking, money moving). Users appear as avatars,
trust badges, and proof-of-purchase photos. Avoid 3D renders, glassmorphism, and corporate
stock photography.

## 7. Application

Foreground the contributor and the verdict. Orange is a scalpel, not a wash: use it for the one
primary action on a screen. Keep the app light and dense; keep the landing dark and bold.
